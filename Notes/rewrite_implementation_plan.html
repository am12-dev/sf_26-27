03 Four defects found while profiling

All four were found by auditing the specialist’s variables against the raw NHANES files. The first one invalidates published results; the other three change what the rewrite has to do.

1 — Two of the six cycles are the same women

invalidates published AUCs fixed by dropping a cycle

CYCLES contained both 2017_2018 (letter J) and 2017_2020 (prefix P_). The NHANES pre-pandemic release is 2017–2018 combined with the partial 2019–March 2020 sample: field work stopped in March 2020, so NCHS pooled the two to restore national representativeness. The 2017–2018 respondents are physically inside the P_ files. CDC sanctions pooling the pre-pandemic files with 1999–2016, not with 2017–2018.

Sequence numbers do not catch it. DEMO_J runs SEQN 93703–102956; P_DEMO runs 109263–124822. The ranges are disjoint, so SEQN.duplicated() returns nothing. Three independent checks establish the overlap:

  • All 176 cohort women from 2017_2018 match a 2017_2020 woman exactly on femoral-neck BMD, total-hip BMD, age, weight and height. The same test between 2009–2010 and either cycle returns 0.
  • Whole-file: 7,585 of 7,585 distinct BMXWT+BMXHT+BMXBMI+BMXWAIST fingerprints in BMX_J also occur in P_BMX.
  • The matched pairs agree 176 of 176 on bmi, diabetes, any_prior_fracture, steroid_use, postmenopausal, years_since_menopause and ever_used_estrogen.
What the duplication cost

The cross-validation leaked. StratifiedKFold splits rows, not people. For 161 postmenopausal women an identical row could sit in a training fold while its twin was scored in the test fold — exact-duplicate leakage across 12.7% of the cohort. That inflates every cross-validated AUC, including the nested-CV figure described in CLAUDE.md as the defensible one. The published 0.810 and 0.806 are superseded.

Ruling — drop the cycle, recover serum by crosswalk

Both cycles were listed for one reason: serum vitamin D. Among the 176 pairs the J copy carries 173 serum values and the P_ copy 0 — VID was never released for the pre-pandemic file. So 2017_2018 leaves CYCLES, and for the vitamin D arm only the VID_J values are grafted onto their matched P_ records on the BMD+age+weight+height fingerprint. 175 pairs match, 172 carry serum, lifting serum coverage from 815 back to 972. This is a non-standard step and is named as such in methods, with the match rule and the recovered count stated.

The apply_filters assert moves from 1,484 to 1,308, and the cohort assert to 1,111. A failing assert here is the assert working, not a bug.

2 — Global refused-code replace destroys real data

defect fixed by rewrite

REFUSED = [7, 9, 77, 99, 777, 999, 7777, 9999] at OsteoporosisML.py:90 is applied to every numeric column. But 7 and 9 are legitimate values for many variables.

ColumnWhat is deleted
SLD010H / SLD012women sleeping 7 h or 9 h — the largest single loss, and the reason sleep looked sparse
OSQ180 / OSQ210mothers and fathers whose hip fracture happened at age 77 or 99. 149 non-null values, 2 of them the real 999 sentinel → 147; the global list strips it to 144.

Fix. Each variable declares its own plausible range and its own sentinel set in the spec; anything outside is missing. Nothing global. The age-at-event columns get an explicit rule: 999 only.

The claim in CLAUDE.md that sleep “is sparse across cycles and was dropped for data density” describes this bug, not NHANES. With a plausible-range rule, sleep covers 1,107 of 1,111.

3 — Label-decoded and numeric cycles handled inconsistently

defect ×5 one shared decoder

The raw CSVs were exported with value labels applied for most cycles, but the P_* files are numeric. A yes/no read that matches only on strings silently drops every respondent in 2017–2020; one that matches only on numbers drops the other four cycles. This has now surfaced five times:

VariableEffect
DPQ010–090PHQ-9 summed arithmetically over label strings → five cycles become NaN. Decoded, PHQ-9 reaches 1,110 of 1,111.
MCQ195arthritis type unread for 2017–2020 → 19 RA cases lost
MCQ191arthritis type never read at all for 2009–2010 → 16 RA cases lost
OSQ150 / OSQ170 / OSQ200P_OSQ is numeric while five cycles are labelled. A string-only match drops all 264 women in 2017–2020 — reproduced live while preparing this document.

Fix. One shared decoder in block C, applied to every yes/no item, accepting "Yes"/"No", 1/2 and their float forms, and returning NaN for everything else. The per-variable fixes are retired. A load-time assertion checks that every cycle contributes a non-zero count to every decoded variable — a silent whole-cycle drop becomes a loud failure.

4 — Zero written as a denormal float in the 2017–2020 files

defect sanitised on load

171 columns across the P_* CSVs encode zero as 5.397605346934028e-79. It reaches DR2TVD, PAD680 and the DPQ items.

In a plain sum this is harmless, so it does not explain any published number. It breaks the moment anything takes a log, forms a ratio, or tests == 0 — all of which this plan does. Fix: on load, snap |x| < 1e-10 to exactly 0 for these files, guarding against turning NaN into 0 in the process.

Only defect 1 invalidates a result

Defects 2, 3 and 4 touch variables that were never in the trained feature set, or move a handful of values, or were already mostly absent. Defect 1 is different in kind: it duplicated 12.7% of the cohort across cross-validation folds. Any statement of the form “the model reaches 0.810” must be withdrawn until the first clean run.

Q3: the 41 are a missing-data exclusion, and methods must say so

They are statistically indistinguishable from the retained cohort — mean T −1.155 against −1.140. They are excluded because their status could not be determined, not because they were found to be premenopausal. Stating it the other way round would misdescribe the cohort. Their characteristics go in the attrition output.

The 156 still-cycling women are a different case. They cluster at the floor of the age window, their bone is roughly 0.67 T-score units healthier, and years since menopause is undefined for 100% of them — a woman who has not had her last period has no last period to count from. NHANES carries no cycle-regularity item in any of these cycles, so “perimenopausal” cannot be separated from “premenopausal”; they are described as not yet postmenopausal, which is the strongest claim the data supports.

Q4: no sensitivity run on the full cohort

Version 1 recommended running 1,272 as primary and 1,484 as a pre-specified sensitivity arm. The ruling is option A only. Including the not-yet-postmenopausal women would not mean imputing a missing value; it would mean fabricating a menopause date for every one of them, for a group that is nearly event-free on the endpoint the study exists to predict. A limitations line records that the excluded 197 were never used. This removes one full run from the build.

Q7: the rheumatoid arthritis arm excludes a non-random group

Of 1,111 women, 529 report arthritis; 122 of those cannot say which type. With the MCQ191 and MCQ195 reads fixed, RA resolves for 988 women with 71 cases — not 49. The 123 who remain unclassifiable have mean T −0.871, better than the arm they are excluded from (−1.174). The arm therefore carries a harder case mix than the main cohort and its AUC is not comparable to the headline. That sentence goes wherever the arm appears. At T ≤ −2.5 the 71 cases contain 5 events, which is why the ruling is descriptive-only at that cutoff.

06 Imputation policy

Median means the 50th percentile of observed values, not the midpoint of the range

The 22 women missing years_since_menopause receive the median of the 1,089 observed values, not the midpoint of the range. The distinction is large for skewed variables. And the median is recomputed inside each cross-validation fold, from that fold’s training rows only — computing it once over the whole dataset leaks test information into training. The existing _pipe already does this correctly and is kept.

The principle: scattered nonresponse is imputed, structural absence is not. For the structural variables, imputation would mean inventing one cycle’s values from other cycles’ women, who differ in era, instrument and population.

Version 1 proposed an unknown level for RA. The ruling replaces it with a subset.

An unknown level keeps all 1,111 women but lets the model learn from a category that mixes “no arthritis” with “arthritis, type unrecalled” — and those 123 women have systematically better bone. The subset is the honest construction, at the cost of saying plainly that it is not comparable to the headline.

07 The analysis cohorts

Each model is reported on the largest cohort its variables permit. These cohorts branch rather than nest. Only C0 contains all the others: C1 and C2 are parallel restrictions discarding different cycles — C1 drops 2005–06 for want of activity and supplement data, C2 loses the 2017–20 women with no crosswalk twin. 136 women sit in C1 but not C2, and 179 in C2 but not C1.

Only within-cohort comparisons are valid

An AUC on C1 and an AUC on C2 describe different populations and must not be read as a progression or compared to each other. Every comparison that carries a claim — the ablation grid, the vitamin D arms, the diabetes strata — holds one cohort fixed and varies only the feature set.

No cohort clears the osteoporosis target

The best case is C0 at 4.5 events per predictor. Deduplication cost 15 osteoporosis events and took an already thin target thinner. This is exactly what the §09 Riley gate exists to enforce: T ≤ −2.5 is carried by thresholding the T-score regression, which uses all 1,111 women, and the 76-case classifier is retained in METHODS as a labelled comparison rather than presented as a result.

08 Derivations that need care

The vitamin D crosswalk — new, and non-standard

Dropping 2017_2018 removes 157 postmenopausal women who have a serum 25(OH)D value, because VID was never released for the pre-pandemic file. The crosswalk recovers them by matching each J record to its P_ twin.

fingerprint = femoral_neck_bmd + total_hip_bmd + age + weight_kg + height_cm match = exact, inner join, J -> P_ 175 pairs, 172 carrying serum apply = only where P_ serum is null; only for the vitamin D arm result = serum coverage 815 -> 972 (of 1,111)
  • Runs after the cycle drop, on the P_ rows only, and never overwrites an observed value.
  • Emits vitd_crosswalk_audit.csv: the match rule, pairs found, values transferred, and any P_ record matching more than one J record (currently none).
  • Named explicitly in methods. A reviewer must be able to see exactly what was joined and on what.
  • Uses BMD in the match key, which is target-derived. This is a record-linkage key, not a feature — but it means the crosswalk must run before define_features and its intermediate columns must never reach the feature matrix.

The shared yes/no decoder

One function, applied to every yes/no item, replacing five bespoke fixes. Its absence is §03 defect 3.

decode_yn(series) -> float64 "Yes" | 1 | 1.0 -> 1.0 "No" | 2 | 2.0 -> 0.0 everything else -> NaN # incl. "Don't know", "Refused", 7, 9 assert: every cycle contributes a non-zero observed count -> a silent whole-cycle drop becomes a loud failure

Menopause type and years since menopause — Q4

RHQ060 is age at last menstrual period, which for a hysterectomy is the surgery date. If the ovaries were retained, oestrogen production continued past that date, so years-since-menopause overstates true oestrogen-deficient exposure — the quantity that actually drives bone loss. The ruling is to compute it for everyone and carry the type alongside:

menopause_type natural no hysterectomy recorded surgical, ovaries out hysterectomy + oophorectomy surgical, ovaries kept hysterectomy, no oophorectomy unknown hysterectomy status unresolved menopause_natural REBUILT - currently flags only a fraction of the women it should, because it was derived from the hysterectomy flag alone

The confound is measurable, which is why the type variable earns its place: women whose hysterectomy kept the ovaries average nearly twice as many nominal “postmenopausal” years as naturally menopausal women, yet have better bone density.

Estrogen duration, three routes — Q5

Duration is spread across three instrument blocks and must be coalesced, not read from one:

estrogen_years = first non-null of RHQ560Q / RHQ560U pills RHQ576Q / RHQ576U patch RHQ586Q / RHQ586U combined unit column U: "months" -> divide by 12 coverage 345 of 485 ever-users (71%) median 3.0 years levels never 624 | <2y 103 | >=2y 242 | duration unknown 140

Reading only one route was errata 2 — it undercounted usable durations by roughly a fifth. The four-level variable is defined on the full 1,111 so that no one is silently dropped for having an unrecorded duration.

Per-variable sentinels, replacing the global list

Each variable declares its own plausible range and its own missing codes. The age-at-event columns are the worst case under the current global rule:

OSQ180 / OSQ210 age at parent's hip fracture valid 20..110 missing 999 only # NOT 77, NOT 99 - those are real ages global list gives 144 usable; correct rule gives 147 SLD010H / SLD012 hours of sleep valid 2..14 missing 77, 99 # 7 and 9 are the two commonest answers

MET-min/week

Unchanged from version 1 and validated against both of the specialist’s references, which agree on the convention:

met_min_week = 8 × (PAQ610 × PAD615) vigorous work + 4 × (PAQ625 × PAD630) moderate work + 4 × (PAQ640 × PAD645) walk / bicycle transport + 8 × (PAQ655 × PAD660) vigorous recreation + 4 × (PAQ670 × PAD675) moderate recreation
  • Gate-conditional zero fill. A sub-domain left blank while others are valid counts as zero, not missing — the WHO GPAQ rule, not an invention.
  • Validity range 10–960 min per item, 1–7 days.
  • Units. Both papers report MET-hours/week; output both.
  • 2005–06 cannot be rescued. PAQ_D uses the pre-GPAQ instrument with no domain structure. Hence the cycle drop rather than a mapping.

Unit conversions and the comparator

  • Serum 25(OH)D: LBXVIDMS nmol/L ÷ 2.496 → ng/mL; coalesce with LBDVIDMS.
  • Vitamin D intake: mcg × 40 → IU/day. Sitting: PAD680 min ÷ 60 → h/day.
  • OST comparator, unchanged: 0.2 × (weight_kg − age). Reporting an AUC without it is uninterpretable.

The Riley gate — Q2

Before any arm is modelled, its target is checked against a Riley et al. minimum sample size computed for that target, cohort and feature count. An arm that clears it is modelled and reported with an AUC; an arm that does not is reported descriptively, with the shortfall stated. This is what turns “run prediction models for both sites” into something defensible on a cohort with 76 osteoporosis events, and it applies uniformly — site arms, the RA subset, the diabetic stratum.

The CV must now split on people, not rows

Deduplication removes the known duplication, but the guard should be structural. Every cross-validated fit carries a group key so that no two records from the same respondent can straddle a fold boundary, and a load-time check asserts the fingerprint is unique. The failure mode in §03 defect 1 was invisible for six cycles; it should not be able to recur silently.

Carried forward from the current methodology

The forest’s in-sample AUC is 1.000 by construction, so the leaderboard reports train, test and OOB side by side — do not rank by the train/test gap. RF_MIN_SAMPLES_LEAF = 1 stays: nested CV showed raising it lowers test AUC monotonically. Tree importances stay out of reporting in favour of permutation importance. EXPECTED_SIGNS gains no diabetes entry (Q8) and keeps serum_vitd_nmol and total_vitd_mcg at −1, both of which are expected to trip (Q9).

10 Analysis arms

A — Primary. Full feature set, C0 Q1

All eight models, nested cross-validation, against OST with a bootstrap ΔAUC confidence interval. The binary is primary and T ≤ −1.0 is the headline; classifiers lead the tables and the T-score regressors are reported alongside. This is the only number that gets quoted as the study result.

What “nested cross-validation” means here

Fitting eight models and reporting the best is biased upward, because the test data helped pick the winner. Nested CV puts the choosing inside the scoring: an outer loop of 5 folds holds out one fold at a time, and an inner loop of 3 folds — run only on the remaining data — selects the model. The inner loop never sees the outer held-out fold.

On the old cohort this took the osteoporosis figure from 0.833 to 0.806, which is why 0.833 is never quoted. Both numbers are now superseded anyway.

B — Ablation. With and without each variable

Leave-one-variable-out: refit the full panel with one column removed and report the change in out-of-fold AUC. Repeated 5-fold CV, not nested, so the grid is affordable; bootstrap intervals only for variables that actually move AUC. The grid runs once, on the lowest-of-two-sites target (Q2) — running it per site would triple the cost for a comparison nobody asked for.

This is the arm that judges diabetes and family history. Both are expected to add little; saying so in advance is what makes a null result a finding rather than a disappointment.

Borderline / unknown30———

The direction is inverted, and it is body weight: diabetics carry 5.5 kg/m² more BMI, and BMI drives BMD. Adjusted for age and BMI the association disappears. This is the documented type-2 diabetes bone paradox — normal-to-high BMD with higher fracture risk, through bone quality and falls rather than density. The specialist is right about fracture risk; DXA T-score is the wrong instrument to see it.

The arm is built as:

  • Diabetes stays a predictor in the main models, judged by its ablation ΔAUC, not by the sign of its coefficient.
  • No diabetes entry in EXPECTED_SIGNS — adding one on clinical intuition would fire the leak alarm on a correct finding.
  • Descriptive side-by-side plus a diabetes×BMI interaction test on the full 1,111.
  • Stratified modelling in non-diabetics only; the diabetic stratum is descriptive at both cutoffs (5 osteoporosis events).
  • The 30 borderline fold into non-diabetic, with a reclassification sensitivity run.
  • A pre-specified −0.5 SD T-score offset sensitivity arm for diabetic women, to show what the paradox would do to referral if it were priced in.
  • Methods states that DIQ010 cannot separate type 1 from type 2.

The BMD-under-detects-fracture caveat goes near the front of the limitations register, not buried in it.

D — Vitamin D, two separate arms Q9

The two measures are missing in complementary cycles

Serum was absent for all of 2017–2020 before the crosswalk; intake is absent for all of 2005–2006, which has no supplement questionnaire. Comparing a serum AUC with an intake AUC compares survey eras, not measures. The two arms therefore run separately, each against its own internal baseline, and are never cross-compared.

  • Serum arm on C2 (n = 972, after crosswalk): full panel with and without serum_vitd_ngml, constant n.
  • Intake arm on C1 (n = 929): full panel with and without vitd_intake_IU, constant n.
  • Neither variable enters the headline model. Serum covers 972 of 1,111; carrying it in the main feature set would mean median-imputing a fifth of a variable that shows no signal.

The near-zero correlations — serum −0.012 (p 0.70), intake −0.012 (p 0.73), calcium +0.009 (p 0.77), against weight +0.501 and BMI +0.456 — are stated in advance as the expectation. The specialist nominated serum expecting it to carry weight and should know beforehand that it does not, in this cohort, at this age range.

E — Depression and sleep, continuous and categorical Q6

Continuous phq9_score and sleep_hrs in the primary models. The categorical versions — phq9_ge10 and the five sleep bands — run as substitution arms: the continuous variable is removed and the categorical put in its place. Never both in one model. A score and its own dichotomisation are collinear by construction and make both coefficients uninterpretable.

Sleep bands on the 1,107: ≤5 h 109 · 6 h 217 · 7–8 h 672 (reference) · 9 h 72 · ≥10 h 37. Every band is usable; none needs collapsing.

F — Site sensitivity Q2

Femoral-neck-only and total-hip-only targets, headline models only, pre-specified as sensitivity analyses rather than a second leaderboard. Each is Riley-gated independently. The lowest-of-two remains the primary definition throughout.

G — Rheumatoid arthritis subset Q7

C4 (n = 988, 71 cases). Modelled at T ≤ −1.0 if it clears the Riley gate; descriptive only at T ≤ −2.5, where the 71 cases hold 5 events. Every table carrying this arm also carries the exclusion statement from §05.

H — Weighting needs restating

The specialist asked for weighted and unweighted results compared. That comparison is not constructible for this cycle set, and the plan says so rather than quietly dropping it. Three reasons compound:

  1. The two-year cycles carry WTMEC2YR; the pre-pandemic file carries WTMECPRP, built on a 3.2-year basis. They are not on a common footing.
  2. The cycles are non-contiguous — 2011–2012 absent, 2015–2016 deliberately excluded. NHANES pooled-weight guidance covers contiguous cycles sharing a design stratification; there is no sanctioned formula for gapped sets.
  3. Two cycles overlapped, and no weighting scheme repairs double-counted respondents.

What is offered instead: unweighted prediction work as the primary — the goal is ranking individual patients, not estimating prevalence — plus within-cycle weighted descriptive estimates reported per cycle rather than pooled. That answers the spirit of the request, showing whether the sampled women differ from the population they were drawn from, without asserting a pooled weight that does not exist.

12 How the code is laid out

One file, run the same way. Internally reorganised into seven blocks, with the variable spec as a declarative table at the top so the whole contract is readable in one screen.

OsteoporosisML.py A CONFIG CYCLES (5), seeds, thresholds, reference means/SDs B VARIABLE SPEC the allowlist: name, source, type, valid range, sentinels, derivation, transform, impute rule, cohort tier C LOAD per-cycle read, denormal sanitise, per-variable missing codes, shared yes/no decoder, per-cycle count assertions D DERIVE vitamin D crosswalk, MET, alcohol, RA, PHQ-9, calcium, menopause_type, estrogen duration, family history E QC duplicate fingerprint check, distributions, cohort definitions, Riley gate, Table 1 F MODEL panel, group-aware nested CV, ablation grid, arms A-H G REPORT save_csv by tier, figures, limitations

Five structural changes

  • Inclusion, not exclusion. define_features currently subtracts a hand-maintained exclude list from every column, so anything BMD-derived leaks unless remembered. Block B is an allowlist — leakage becomes impossible rather than guarded against. This matters more now that the crosswalk puts BMD into a join key.
  • Per-variable missing codes. Each variable declares its valid range and sentinels; the global REFUSED replace goes away.
  • One shared yes/no decoder, with a per-cycle non-zero assertion behind it.
  • Group-aware cross-validation. A respondent key travels with every fold split, and a fingerprint-uniqueness assert runs at load.
  • Encoding inside the pipeline. A ColumnTransformer joins imputation, encoding and scaling so all three are fold-local.

Dependencies

statsmodels is not installed; scipy is. Adjusted regressions use scipy or a numpy least-squares implementation rather than adding a dependency. Nothing new to install, so a fresh clone still runs offline on the committed download caches. Note that dropping 2017_2018 does not remove its cached files — they stay on disk and stay committed, since the crosswalk reads VID_J.

14 Build order

Sequential, because each step’s output is the next step’s input. Steps 0–5 are verifiable against the numbers in this document before any model runs.

  1. Drop the duplicated cycle

    Remove 2017_2018 from CYCLES; add the fingerprint-uniqueness check and emit duplicate_check.csv; re-pin the DXA assert. Checkpoint: 1,308 records, zero duplicate fingerprints, attrition chain 56,769 / 28,644 / 10,672 / 1,639 / 1,308.

  2. Variable spec and loader

    Block B as a declarative table; per-variable ranges and sentinels replace the global refused list; shared yes/no decoder with per-cycle assertions; denormal sanitising on the P_* files. Checkpoint: sleep reaches 1,107 and PHQ-9 reaches 1,110; OSQ180 gives 147, not 144.

  3. Cohort filter and assert

    Menopause filter after the inclusion chain; re-pin the cohort assert at 1,111; emit the attrition table with both excluded groups characterised. Checkpoint: 645 and 76 events; cycle counts 173 / 228 / 227 / 219 / 264.

  4. Derivations

    Vitamin D crosswalk first (it needs the raw J files); then MET with the GPAQ rules, alcohol across both instruments, RA subset, menopause type, estrogen duration, family history. Checkpoint: serum 815 → 972; RA 71 cases on 988; parental hip fx 167; estrogen duration 345 of 485.

  5. Distribution QC

    The specialist’s pre-analysis requirement: per-variable distributions, skew, zero-inflation, histogram panel, recommended transform. Checkpoint: matches §05.

  6. Cohort definitions, Riley gate and Table 1

    C0–C5 with events per cohort; Riley minimum computed per target and cohort; demographic table in the specialist’s bands. Checkpoint: C0 1,111 · C1 929 · C2 972 · C3 793 · C4 988 · C5 711.

  7. Pipeline and model panel

    ColumnTransformer for impute/encode/scale; the eight models; group-aware folds; OST comparator. Leaderboard with train, test and OOB.

  8. Nested CV headline

    Arm A on C0, binary primary, with bootstrap ΔAUC against OST. This is the number that replaces 0.810.

  9. Ablation grid

    Arm B: leave-one-variable-out across all eight models, once, on lowest-of-two. Bootstrap intervals only where AUC moves.

  10. Stratified, paired and subset arms

    Arms C through H — diabetes with the interaction test and the offset arm, the two vitamin D arms, depression and sleep substitutions, site sensitivity, the RA subset, within-cycle weighted descriptives.

  11. Reporting

    All tables via save_csv with tiers, forest plot, calibration curve, limitations register.

  12. Documentation

    Update CLAUDE.md — five cycles, the 1,308 and 1,111 asserts, the crosswalk, the defect fixes, and the withdrawal of the 0.810/0.806 results table. Update Notes/project_walkthrough.html so the explainer matches. Both currently describe a cohort that no longer exists.
